Cognate Detection Rules

A word is marked as a transparent cognate if it matches any of these patterns when comparing the Spanish word to its English translation:

Suffix Transformations

Spanish suffixes that map to English equivalents:

-ción → -tion
información → information nación → nation educación → education
-ista → -ist
artista → artist pianista → pianist terrorista → terrorist
-dad → -ty
universidad → university realidad → reality identidad → identity
-mente → -ly
normalmente → normally exactamente → exactly finalmente → finally
-oso/-osa → -ous
famoso → famous nervioso → nervous deliciosa → delicious
-ar → -ate
celebrar → celebrate separar → separate
-ado/-ada → -ated / -ed
activado → activated preparado → prepared

Phonetic Matching

English digraphs are normalized to Spanish equivalents before comparing:

ph → f, th → t
euforia → euphoria farmacia → pharmacy teatro → theater

Why Exclude Cognates?

Transparent cognates are "free" vocabulary - you likely already recognize them from English. Excluding them lets you focus study time on words that actually require memorization.

Switch to Extra vocabulary?

Extra is a second, supplementary set of vocabulary — separate from the Main deck you've been studying.

It collects the words that don't recur across the lyrics:

  • Words that appear only once
  • Loanwords and English code-switches
  • Names, places, and slang

It's grouped by kind rather than by frequency, and it's best tackled once you've mostly finished Main. Your Main progress stays exactly where it is — you can switch back any time.

    Review & Progress

    How reviews are timed

    You forget a new word within a day or two. You forget a word you have known for months far more slowly. Reviews are timed to land just before you would have forgotten.

    1. Get a word rightIt waits longer before coming back — a day, then a few days, then a couple of weeks, and so on.
    2. Get it wrongIt comes back soon, and the waits start growing again from there.
    3. Keep getting it rightThe gaps stretch to months. You stop spending time on words you already know.

    That is the whole idea: see each word about as often as you need to, and no more. Turning it off means nothing comes back on a schedule — only the words you actually got wrong stay in your review queue.

    What is this?
    
                    
    This app teaches vocabulary from either subtitle speech or an artist's lyrics, starting with the most useful words.
    
                    
    Why frequency order?
    
                    
    Language follows a power law: a small number of words make up the vast majority of speech. By learning the most frequent words first, you understand more lyrics faster.
    
                    
    How are percentages calculated?
    
                    
    The coverage percentage tells you what fraction of all words in the lyrics you'd recognize. For example, at 70% coverage you know enough words to understand roughly 70% of the words in any song. The remaining 30% are rarer words that appear less often.
    
                    
    How does it work?
    
                    
    Choose a language, then pick Everyday Speech or Music & Lyrics. Select a level to learn bite-sized sets of the most common words. Any cards you miss stay in review so you can master them.
    
                    
    The progress bar tracks your coverage based on the frequency of words you've learned — learning a common word contributes more to your coverage than a rare one.
    
                    
    
                        
                    
    
                
    
            
    
            
            
    
                
    
                    
    Picking a set: Choose Everyday Speech or Music & Lyrics, then pick a level. Fluency automatically highlights your next unfinished set. Turn on Fast Track so related forms share one card and obvious English look-alikes are set aside.
    
                    
    Learning and review: Learn new contains only cards you have not attempted. Incorrect or partly learned cards move into the selected level's separate Review queue. Correct recalls return after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, and 120 days; mistakes reset the schedule.
    
                    
    Continuing: If you leave an unfinished set, a Welcome back prompt offers to restore the exact card, order, card side, and settings next time you enter. Finishing the set clears it.
