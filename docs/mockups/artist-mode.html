Artist mode: deck first, songs later

Mock-up only, nothing is wired up. Idea: choosing an artist takes you straight to that artist's whole deck. Narrowing to certain songs becomes an optional filter you can change any time on the deck page, not a gate you must pass first.

Why this is less tiring

  • One decision per screen. Who → (optionally) which songs → go. Today you decide who and which songs before you see anything.
  • Default is everything. Most people just want the artist's vocabulary by frequency; the filter is there for "only the new album" or "only songs I know".
  • The chip shows the state. "Songs: all 312" vs "Songs: 3 ✎" tells you at a glance what you are studying, so nothing is hidden in a side panel.
  • One way back. A single "‹ Music" link. Playlists and Series are sibling tabs on the pick screen, not extra buttons on every screen.
  • Same page shape as Home (Learn left, Practise right), so artist mode stops feeling like a different app.
